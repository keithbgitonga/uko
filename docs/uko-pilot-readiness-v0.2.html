Executive summary

Since v0.1 the product moved from “core flows work” to “pilot-grade engineering”: normalized Postgres persistence with a tested backup/restore drill, dispatch console with audit log and role allow-list, security hardening (headers, request IDs, OTP rate limits, SMS adapter), real-shaped integrations for M-Pesa Daraja, SMS and FCM tested against local gateways, in-app notification inboxes for riders and drivers, ratings, analytics/readiness metrics and a 15-check pilot smoke suite.

Verdict unchanged but better placed: a 4-week internal pilot is viable once the external credentials and operational agreements close. Public beta still needs maps/live location, managed infrastructure, crash/alerting, driver vetting, legal/DPA and a production dispatch shell.

One Nairobi zone · 15–25 riders · 8–12 Bodas · 4 weeks · Boda only · cash-first with M-Pesa after Daraja passes · no cancellation fee · ~20% commission · private responder (no 999 dialling). KPIs: assignment <3 min, completion ≥90%, cancellation ≤10%, zero unresolved safety incidents, CSAT ≥4.5/5, daily reconciliation complete, smoke suite green.
