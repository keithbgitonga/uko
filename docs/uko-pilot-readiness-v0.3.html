Executive summary

The MVP is now operationally ready for an internal pilot: driver document vetting with an online gate, scheduled and recurring rides that actually activate, payment reconciliation, crash reporting from both apps, alerting with Slack-compatible webhooks, document expiry reminders, ratings, notification inboxes and a device-level end-to-end trip on the Lenovo. A 27-check API dry run passes in one pass.

What remains is external or legal: real SMS/FCM/Maps/Daraja credentials, managed Postgres with secrets and TLS, a named safety responder, camera/gallery document capture, and privacy/DPA paperwork. None of these require new product design; they require accounts, agreements and configuration.

One Nairobi zone · 15–25 riders · 8–12 Bodas · 4 weeks · Boda only · cash-first with M-Pesa after Daraja passes · no cancellation fee · ~20% commission · private responder (no 999 dialling). KPIs: assignment <3 min, completion ≥90%, cancellation ≤10%, zero unresolved safety incidents, CSAT ≥4.5/5, daily reconciliation clean, smoke suite green.
