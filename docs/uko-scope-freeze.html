Scope freeze · decision record
Uko? MVP — frozen scope
Boda-first pilot in one Nairobi zone. No cancellation fee. ~20% commission. Recurring rides and calendar suggestions included.
v1.1 · 21 November 2026 · Based on PRD v0.1 and Architecture Delta v0.1 (scope additions: recurring schedules, calendar suggestions)

Decisions locked by the project owner

DecisionValue
Category mixBoda only at pilot. Go, Comfort and XL are deferred until after the pilot.
Commission~20% per completed ride, booked to the ledger; driver payout reports show net earnings. Weekly payout cadence, manual in MVP.
Cancellation feeNone during the pilot. The fee stays a server-owned policy field, set to 0; reliability is still tracked on both sides.
Emergency responsePrivate response model — Uko? ops on-call plus a private responder. No 999 integration in the pilot.
Brand / name“Uko?” confirmed as the product name.
Scheduling window45 minutes to 90 days ahead (pilot default — confirm or shorten).
Recurring schedulesIn the MVP. Weekly pattern (selected weekdays), start/end, pause/resume, edit future, cancel one occurrence or the series. Max 8-week horizon; occurrences generated 14 days ahead; billed per occurrence at activation.
Calendar suggestionsIn the MVP. Opt-in, read-only calendar access; on-device processing; suggestions for events with a location in the next 7 days; no calendar data stored server-side; no write-back.
Impact on the specs: PRD v0.1 and Architecture Delta v0.1 have been updated in place — category, cancellation, commission, emergency and brand now match these decisions, and recurring schedules + calendar suggestions are now part of MVP scope rather than 0.2.
Assumptions to confirm: 45-minute lead time / 90-day advance window; recurring max 8-week horizon and per-occurrence payment at activation; calendar suggestions limited to the next 7 days. All other items in this record are frozen.

Frozen MVP scope

In scope — release 0.1
  • Android rider app, Android driver app, web dispatch console, public track link
  • Phone OTP identity; rider and driver profiles; driver documents and approval
  • On-demand booking — Boda category only, upfront quote, ETA, matching
  • Scheduled rides — single booking, 45 min–90 days, prepaid, pre-assigned, reminded
  • Recurring schedules — weekly pattern, series management, occurrences generated ahead
  • Calendar suggestions — opt-in, on-device, pre-fill scheduling from events
  • Driver offers, scheduled request list, navigation handoff, trip state machine, pickup code
  • MQTT tracking with polling fallback; share-trip link
  • M-Pesa STK and cash payments; 20% commission; receipts; ledger
  • SOS, incident reporting, trusted contacts, masked calls
  • Ratings, receipts, support cases, trip history
  • Dispatch: live map, rides board, schedule calendar, assign/reassign, refunds, incidents
  • English + Swahili; EAT; low-bandwidth behaviour
Deferred
  • Go / Comfort / XL categories
  • Card payments and 3-D Secure (0.2)
  • Automated payouts (0.2)
  • Ride-check automation (0.2)
  • Audio recording / speech-to-text (0.3)
  • Price bidding and counter-offers (0.3)
  • Calendar write-back / smarter event parsing (V0.2)
  • Corporate/work profiles, driver rental/lending
  • iOS and additional cities
Boda-only keeps the pilot simple: one category, one fare rule set, one supply pool.
